# Product Vision

**Status: WORKING**  
**Milestone: Research Foundation v0.1**

## Vision

Build **Cinema and Series** into a living, source-transparent knowledge database for world cinema and television, with exceptionally deep coverage of Indian cinema, from cinema history through productions currently being made and publicly announced future projects.

The product must be capable of growing from a high-integrity metadata database into a broader cinema information platform without replacing its foundational identity, provenance, release, localization, or history models.

## The problem

Current products solve valuable but fragmented problems: broad title metadata, ratings, cinephile diaries, streaming availability, theatrical ticketing, reviews, episode tracking, or discovery. None should be assumed to provide the exact combination Cinema and Series needs:

- worldwide movie and series knowledge;
- unusually strong Indian-language and regional-cinema modeling;
- native-script and romanized identity;
- clean distinction between originals, dubs, remakes, re-releases, alternate cuts, and adaptations;
- production lifecycle for announced and in-production works;
- territory-specific release history rather than one release-date field;
- source-level provenance and conflict handling;
- transparent change history;
- an architecture that is not dependent on one external database.

## Product promise

Cinema and Series should aim for **maximum measurable coverage**, not an unverifiable claim of literal 100% completeness.

The system must clearly distinguish:

- known;
- unknown;
- missing;
- disputed;
- stale;
- inferred candidate data;
- verified canonical data.

## Strategic differentiation

The long-term product position is:

> Global cinema knowledge, India-deep data modeling, multilingual identity, production and release lifecycle, and evidence-backed metadata.

India is not modeled as "Bollywood plus regional films." Telugu, Tamil, Malayalam, Kannada, Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Punjabi, Assamese, Odia, Gujarati, Bhojpuri, and other cinema traditions must be representable as first-class language/market ecosystems while still participating in one global model.

## Long-term potential

If the database foundation proves reliable, later layers may include release tracking, OTT availability, personal library/watch history, ratings and reviews, lists, recommendations, news, box office, community features, creator/industry tooling, and a public API.

These later layers must remain consumers of the canonical data platform rather than forcing the core model to become a feature-specific schema.
